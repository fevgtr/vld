What this is

This is my Virtual Learning Development (VLD) project for the 2026–27 school year.
VLD gives me one period a week to teach myself something outside the normal
timetable, with a supervisor checking in on how self-directed I’m being. This
site is where I keep track of it.

What I’m learning

Data structures and algorithms, in C++.

The course I’m following is Data Structures (数据结构) from Tsinghua University,
taught by Prof. Deng Junhui, hosted free on XuetangX. It’s the real course their
CS department teaches, not a summary of it. Everything is in C++, and the
programming assignments are graded automatically by Tsinghua’s own online judge.

  • Course, Part 1: https://www.xuetangx.com/course/THU08091000384
  • Online judge: https://dsa.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/oj/

Grading for the course is 6 problem sets (60%) plus 4 programming assignments (40%).
My plan for this year is to finish Part 1 — vectors, lists, stacks and queues,
binary trees, and graphs. Part 2 can wait until I’ve actually got through Part 1.

Why this, and why C++

I want to study computer science, and data structures is the thing everything else
sits on top of. I’ve written a fair amount of C++ already for competitive
programming, but that kind of code is all loops and arrays — I’ve never really
built a templated class or thought about why one container is faster than another.
This course is built entirely around that, so it fills in exactly the part I’ve
been skipping.

It’s also something I can’t cram. That’s sort of the point of giving it a year.

How this site is organised

  • Home / All Entries — my learning journal, one entry per week.
  • Resources — the course links, the judge, the textbook, and anything else I’m using.

Entries are dated. Some weeks are going to be more honest than impressive, and I’d
rather write down where I got stuck than pretend I didn’t.
