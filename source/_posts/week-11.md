## 2. What was challenging, and how I handled it

The 2-Subset problem looks simple: given a set of integers, find a subset that sums to a target. But no one has found a polynomial-time algorithm for it. The lecture walks through why brute force grows exponentially with the set size, since you have to check 2^n possible subsets.

C4 then showed how to compare growth rates formally. Seeing 2^n next to n² and n log n on the same scale made it clear why polynomial vs. exponential is the dividing line from C2 and C3.
