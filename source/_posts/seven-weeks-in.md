## Where that leaves me in the course

Still inside Chapter 1, 绪论. I've worked through 计算 and most of 计算模型 —
performance measurement, problem size, worst case, the idealised model, and the
Turing machine including the worked example (01B2-2). Still ahead in this chapter:
the RAM model, big-O notation, and then iteration and recursion.

That's slower than I expected. One period a week turns out to be about two or
three lecture videos plus the exercises attached to them, and the exercises are
where the time actually goes.

## Being straight about the journal

The site went up today, which means the entries for weeks 2, 3 and 5 are being
written after the fact rather than on the day. I'd rather say that than pretend
otherwise. I've kept the scope of each session, so the facts are right, but the
reflections are reconstructed and I know that's weaker than writing them fresh.

From here the entry gets written in the session, not weeks later.
