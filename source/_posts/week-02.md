## 2. What was challenging, and how I handled it

I expected code and got geometry. The rope and straightedge examples seemed random, but they're about what counts as a "basic operation" in different models. You can't analyse an algorithm until you agree what one step is. The four algorithm properties (correctness, determinism, feasibility, finiteness) gave me trouble too. I kept mixing up feasibility and finiteness until the Hailstone sequence example made it click. A program that might run forever isn't an algorithm, even if every step is simple.
