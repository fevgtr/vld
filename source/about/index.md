## What this is

This is my Virtual Learning Development (VLD) project for the 2026–27 school year.
VLD gives me one period a week to teach myself something outside the normal
timetable, with a supervisor checking in on how self-directed I'm being. This
site is where I keep track of it.

## What I'm learning

**Data structures and algorithms, in C++.**

The course I'm following is *Data Structures* (数据结构) from Tsinghua University,
taught by Prof. Deng Junhui, hosted free on XuetangX. It's the full course their
CS department teaches. Everything is in C++, and the programming assignments are
graded automatically by Tsinghua's own online judge.

- Course, Part 1: <https://www.xuetangx.com/course/THU08091000384>
- Online judge: <https://dsa.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/oj/>

Grading for the course is 6 problem sets (60%) plus 4 programming assignments (40%).
My plan for this year is to finish Part 1: vectors, lists, stacks and queues,
binary trees, and graphs.

## Why this, and why C++

I want to study computer science, and data structures is the thing everything else
sits on top of. I've written a fair amount of C++ for competitive programming,
but that was mostly loops and arrays. This course covers templated classes, container
design, and why one data structure is faster than another, which is the part I
want to get solid on.

It's also something that takes time to absorb. That's sort of the point of giving it a year.
