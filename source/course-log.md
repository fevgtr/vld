## Course

Data Structures (数据结构), Prof. Deng Junhui, Tsinghua University. Free on [XuetangX](https://www.xuetangx.com/course/THU08091000384). Lectures in Chinese, code in C++. Programming assignments go to Tsinghua's [online judge](https://dsa.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/oj/).

Chosen on 4 September 2026 (Week 1). No course switches since then.

## Why this course

I wanted graded work, C++ only, and free. Tsinghua was the only option that met all three:

- Stanford CS106B: good materials, but no grading for outside students.
- UIUC Accelerated CS Fundamentals (Coursera): C++, but too short for a full year.
- UC San Diego DSA: mixes Python, Java, and C++, and needs financial aid.
