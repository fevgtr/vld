> This is a Semester 2 deliverable. I'm leaving the structure here so I don't
> forget it, and I'll write it once I've got enough of the course behind me to
> give a Grade 10 student advice worth reading.

The bio is written for Grade 10 students deciding whether to take VLD. It needs
six things:

1. Name or preferred name, and grade
2. One sentence about me as a learner
3. Courses taken through VLD, and why I switched if I did
4. What I hoped to gain
5. A quote or motto I'd actually say out loud
6. The one thing I wish someone had told me in week one

No full legal name, address, phone number, birthdate, ID numbers, social handles,
or photos that show where I live.
